May 11th (Sunday): Five Lake Loop + Downtown Exploring

Activity: Five Lakes Loop

  • Summary: Depart at 6.45am, arrive before 7.30am to avoid full parking. Require timed entry reservation (Bear Lake Corridor permit)
  • Address: Bear Lake Rocky Mountain National Park, Colorado
  • Details: Trail for scenic view. The whole loop is about 10.5 km (shortest loop also need 8km), and high elevation will double your exhaustion. We will NOT do that. We will do the first three lakes, and walk back. About 5 km walk (2.5km + 2.5km). Expect to take 2.5 hours
  • Note: Traction device. Not stroller-friendly. Only has public restroom at the starting spot (parking lot). Bring some snacks + enough water (sunscreen, hats, etc.)
  • Others: If have any questions, we should go to Bear Lake Ranger Station (open at 8am)

Alternatives: If the trail somehow doesn't work, we'll go Sprague Lake, Lily Lake, Alluvial Fan, Deer Mountain, Bierstadt Lake, Loch & Mills Lake Junction, Wild Basin to Ouzel Falls trails instead

Rest: Drive back to get lunch. Rest in the afternoon. If have time before lunch, we can go Moraine Park Discovery Center and Beaver Meadows Visitor Center

Downtown Exploring: Optional. In the evening, walk around downtown/Riverwalk park/Lake Estes. Could explore art galleries too
